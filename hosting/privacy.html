Privacy Policy

Human respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

Human v1 - Strength

Human v1 - Strength is a strength-training application that can be used locally without requiring a cloud account.

Information the app may store

The app may store information you provide or generate while using it, including profile details, workout history, routines, exercise records, body measurements, preferences, and training progress.

Local storage

Workout and profile data may be stored locally on your device so the app can function offline.

Cloud services

If you choose to use cloud features or sign in, Human may use Google Firebase services to provide authentication, synchronisation, backup, and related application functionality.

Google Play purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Google Play. Human may verify subscription status using Google Play services so that access to paid application features can be provided correctly.

Data retention

Expiry or cancellation of a subscription does not automatically delete your workout history, routines, profile, measurements, preferences, or other training data.

Sharing of information

Human does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the application, such as Google Firebase and Google Play.

Your choices

You may use core local functionality without signing into a cloud account. Where cloud functionality is used, account and data-management options will be provided within the application.

Children

Human v1 - Strength is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in breach of applicable law.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated as Human develops and introduces new features or services.
