# Provisional Release Roadmap

Targets count approved canonical catalogue records, not aliases or minor parameter variations.

| Stage | Provisional target | Intent |
|---|---:|---|
| Minimum launch catalogue | Approximately 350–500 | Foundational and expected commercial coverage |
| Strong commercial catalogue | Approximately 700–1,000 | Wider equipment, progression, specialist, and facility coverage |
| Long-term platform catalogue | Approximately 1,200–1,800 | Carefully justified platform breadth |

These are planning ranges, not quotas. Integrity takes priority over record count.

## Gates

Architecture approval precedes a small cross-family pilot. The minimum release focuses on approved Essential candidates; commercial depth follows with Recommended candidates and facility coverage analysis. Extended and Future candidates enter only with clear admission justification.

Production import, UUID allocation, coaching generation, localisation, clinical evidence work, and application release planning remain separate authorised tasks.
